# Compatible local overrides preserve retained handles

Mamba rejects flag/option cross-kind collisions and permits same-kind local overrides only with identical output type and cardinality. Compatible ancestor and descendant handles both read the effective descendant value under the descendant's validation/default rules, rather than making ancestor hooks lose their retained declarations or forbidding all local customization. This refines ADR-0001's effective-spelling/override contract. Accessor overrides preserve every ancestor container/leaf path with compatible kind, output type, and cardinality, may add local paths, and do not implicitly deep-merge omitted declarations. Paired/selected members cannot replace applicable standalone declarations even when scalar types match; groups remain local and their aggregate maps do not establish override compatibility.
